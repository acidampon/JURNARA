JURNARA Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

JURNARA is a career-planning and job-application preparation application. This policy explains how the current version of JURNARA handles information.

Information stored

The current version stores information you enter into the app, such as your name, email address, career goal, skills, CV information, saved opportunities, application tracking information, roadmap progress, and related career-planning data.

Where your information is stored

The current version is local-first and does not require a JURNARA account. The information listed above is stored in the app's local storage on your device. JURNARA does not currently operate a cloud account database or send this information to a JURNARA server.

Sharing and third parties

The current version does not sell your personal information, does not use advertising SDKs, and does not share your locally stored career information with third parties. The app includes standard web and Android runtime components needed to operate the application.

Job and AI features

The current release contains demo opportunity records and a rule-based career coach. It does not currently connect your personal information to an external AI provider or live job-feed service.

Retention and deletion

Your locally stored JURNARA information remains on your device until you edit or delete it, use the app's Reset action, or remove the app and its data according to your device's normal operating-system behavior. JURNARA does not retain a separate server copy of this local-first information in the current release.

Your choices

You can edit information inside the app. The app also provides a Reset action that clears its locally stored JURNARA data from the device. You can also remove the app from your device, subject to the operating system's normal app-data behavior.

Security

Because the current release stores information locally, you should protect access to your device. We do not claim that any storage method is completely risk-free.

Changes to this policy

If JURNARA later adds cloud accounts, live job feeds, analytics, payments, external AI services, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before those features are used in a way that changes the app's data practices.

Developer and privacy contact

JURNARA is the app identified by this policy. For privacy questions or requests, use the public JURNARA repository issue mechanism: JURNARA privacy/support issues. This is the current release's privacy contact mechanism.
